I played Cuphead near launch (October 2017) and loved it. The visuals, the music, the gameplay—a perfect package.


I picked it up again in August 2021, discovered the speedrunning community, and never looked back.


EXPLORE MY CUPHEAD JOURNEY
One weekend in August 2021, my brothers and I had the sudden urge to play Cuphead again. This time around, we were fixated on improving our best S-Rank times on each boss. We had a spreadsheet to keep track of our best times, and we made it a little competition. We didn't really think of it as "speedrunning" in the traditional sense; it was just a fun excuse to play the game again.

Eventually, it got to a point where my S-Rank times felt limited by my own basic knowledge of the game, and so I turned to online resources to learn more about the optimal strategies. So where did I look? Speedrun.com. Specifically, I stumbled upon the Individual Level (IL) scene. Watching those top-level ILs was mindblowing— I could hardly wrap my head around it.

"How do I use Charge?"
"What is that ghost attack??"
"How are they parrying the boss???"

I was obsessed. Let's just say I got a little carried away...
Over the course of 3 years, I managed to beat or tie every IL world record on every ground boss on the current patch of Cuphead. That's 108/108 world records.

To this day, I honestly cannot believe I actually did that.

After I finished my IL arc in late 2024, I shifted my focus toward website development.

The Combined Leaderboard was the first website I made for the Cuphead speedrunning community. It uses the speedrun.com API to fetch runs from the online leaderboards, then does some calculations to determine the best overall players across each category. My vision was to create a high-visibility hub that rewarded players who excelled at multiple categories.

It was a hit! The niche online community of Cuphead speedrunners loved it, and it encouraged players to try new categories to improve their overall ranking. Since its inception, I've added a bunch of other speedgames, and the site has been shared across many other communities.


Runner Resources is a collection of tools, links, and guides for new players to learn about various aspects of Cuphead Speedrunning. It serves as a high-visiblity hub for easy access to a multiude of resources.

The site also has sections displaying boss HP, weapon damage values, and detailed explanations of obscure boss mechanics.


Run Recap! My personal favorite website I've created. This is a Cuphead speedrun analysis tool which provides an in-depth breakdown of completed runs.

Run Recap originated as a side project on the Combined Leaderboard, but it eventually flourished into its own website with a plethora of features, including an exhaustive individual level database. It has seen widespread usage across the community, from top players all around the world.
